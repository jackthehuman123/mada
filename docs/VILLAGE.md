## 1. What the story fixes

- A poor village **on a lake**, hard to reach: a **causeway over the marsh** that floods half the rainy season, or the
  weekly **market boat** to the **dock** (§2). The players walk in along the old causeway at dusk (§13).
- An **old shrine by the water**. On Rằm the village carried lanterns from it **all the way round to the puppet stage on
  the far shore** and set them on the lake; they drift to **the middle and gather** "like a little town made of
  light" (§2). Under them: the **drowned festival** (§10).
- **Lanes:** Sen's house is **at the very end of the last lane**; the teller lived **three doors down** (§3).
  Neighbours hear each other's radios along the lane (§3); people sit on their front steps in the evening (§3).
- **Behind Sen's house:** a back step (§6), a **back yard**, a **fence** her voice came over (§4), and **old stone steps
  down into a cove**: reachable only through that yard, **seen from nowhere in the village**, and from the bottom
  step you see **the whole lake, right across to the far shore** (§4). The puppet master, on his stage, saw a lantern
  drift out **from behind the last house on the lane** (§7).
- **A well** (§5). **Thu's gate** (§12). The **communal house** where the village voted (§11).
- **Inside Sen's house** (§14, §15): no marks on the doorframe; one small bowl and chopsticks at one place, freshly
  washed; the back room with an old woman's chair facing the door and teacups grey with dust; a half-finished paper
  fish on a shelf; the family altar with the ancestors' photographs (the letter folded very small beneath them, §9);
  the old radio, hissing (§15).
- **The steps:** little sandals lined up, one pair per lost child; the carp come up (§14). Sen's father's jacket lies in
  the reeds at the bottom of the lake (§11).
- **The shrine:** the photos the families left, the red paper with every lost child's name but one, Thu's mat among
  the lanterns, tea (§12–§14).
- The village **emptied** after the vote (§11). **Four lost children's houses:** the teacher's (Minh), the puppet
  master's (Hạnh), the fish seller's (Tí), the village head's (Khoa) (§10, §14).

   (SW)                    south bay           village: two roads, east shore
          dark forest . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . karst

- **The lake:** irregular, about 420 × 320 studs. The **marsh arm** in the south-west, the shrine's **stone point** in
  the west, the **south bay** with the dock, the **village shore** in the east, the **headland** in the north-east,
  the **festival ground** on the open north shore.
- **Arrival:** the **earth causeway** crosses the marsh arm to a **gate in the bamboo hedge** (two red lanterns,
  `6_village_edge_graves.jpg`). From the gate, a shore path runs **west and north to the shrine** on its point, and
  **east to the dock**, where a **plank bridge crosses the mouth of the bay** to the village.
- **The village:** a cluster on the east shore, **two roads** running north along the shore:
  - **the lower road** along the water (`3_down_the_lane.jpg`): the low brick wall on the water side, nets, the dock
    end; the busier, lit houses;
  - **the upper road**, one row inland: quieter;
  - both run north into **the last lane** (`4_the_last_lane.jpg`), overgrown, which ends at **Sen's house on the
    headland**. The teller's old house is three doors before it (§3). The **well** stands where the roads meet.
  - The **communal house** sits at the village's south end by the bridge, the first big roof you reach.
- **The headland:** rocks rising out of the water with Sen's house on top. The **mouth of the secret cove** opens
  west under the rocks, toward the middle and the far shore (§4, §7); the steps go down to it from her back yard.
  From the village roads you see the house, never the steps (§4).
- **The far shore (north):** the open festival ground with string lights and tattered banners, the pavilion, and the
  **water-puppet stage on stilts** out in the water. The **flooded path** runs from the headland north round to it:
  you can see it disappear into the water; you can't follow it.
- **The middle:** the lanterns gather between the shrine and the stage; Sen drifts there (I-16); the drowned
  festival is below.
- **All round:** dark forest, then **karst mountains**: sheer limestone peaks that close the valley in. The sky line is
  peaks on every side: there is visibly no way out except the causeway, and it floods.

## 3. Rules the layout keeps (gameplay)

- **Roads are safe-ish, the water is hers.** The lower road runs along the water: walking it alone is tense; the upper
  road is ~35 studs inland, outside Sen's 30-stud reach. The **bridge over the bay**, the **dock**, the shore path to the
  shrine and **the steps** are over or next to water: alone there, she takes you.
- **Houses face the water** (the lower road's directly, the upper road's over the roofs below). Each lost child's
  house has its **lantern stop on the shore below it** (§12). Sen's has none.
- **Sightlines:** from the shrine you see the whole village across the lake, and on Night 1 the lanterns streaming out
  to the houses (`2_from_the_shrine.jpg`): the one dark house is the tall one on the headland at the end. The cove's
  steps are invisible from the roads, the dock and the shrine (§4); its mouth faces the middle and the stage (§7).
- **The shrine is the safe place** (`ShrineZone`): warm, candles, incense, far from the houses on purpose: going back
  there means crossing the bridge and the shore path.
- **No way out:** the causeway floods after Night 1; the hedge gate leads to the fields and graves and the forest,
  where "Wandering in the forest won't save you." brings you back; beyond that, karst cliffs. The flooded path to
  the festival ground is cut.
- Every tag and Location name stays (`Shrine`, `TeacherHouse`, … `CarpSteps`, `Dock`, `Causeway`, `DrownedFestival`);
  positions move.

## 4. The places

**The causeway and the hedge gate.** A narrow raised earth causeway over black marsh water, reeds, dead trunks. It ends
at a gate in the bamboo hedge with two red lanterns, the graves in the long grass beside it. Players spawn here.

**The shrine.** A small open shrine on a stone point, a stone shore wall beside it, steps down into the water lined
with candles and an incense coil (`2_from_the_shrine.jpg`). Inside: the altar, rows of the families' photos, the red
paper (§11), Thu's straw mat, teapot and cups, lanterns finished and half-folded. Bin's camera floats in the reeds just
off the point (§13).

**The dock and the bridge.** A plank bridge across the mouth of the south bay with a jetty off it, a few planks
missing, baskets left on it, a rotting boat beside it. The market boat no longer comes (§2).

**The communal house (đình).** At the village's south end: a raised brick platform, heavy columns, a big tiled roof with
curled corners, an open front, benches set out for the vote (§11), a pond in front.

**The houses** (`3_down_the_lane.jpg`): one storey, limewashed walls in pale ochre, terracotta tile roofs, wooden
shutters and doors, a step at the door, a red lantern; brick yard walls; water jars, firewood, baskets, nets on poles.
- **The village head's:** the best kept, a writing desk, the minutes and the shrine list (§14).
- **The fish seller's:** on the lower road by the dock; baskets, nets, a stall table (§9, §14).
- **The puppet master's:** water puppets hanging from the rafters, paints, his diary (§14).
- **The teacher's:** a slate, a desk with a drawer (the unsent report), the class photo with Sen in it (§14).
- **The teller's old house:** three doors before Sen's (§3), on the last lane; a radio on the table, the dial up (§3).
- **The empty houses:** doors open, bundles left, sandals on a step, a broom, one oil lamp still burning
  (`4_the_last_lane.jpg`).

**The well.** Where the two roads meet: round, brick, a rope and two buckets (§5).

**Sen's house.** On the headland at the very end of the last lane: **two storeys** (`4_the_last_lane.jpg`), the only one,
flaking plaster, shutters closed, standing apart from the others. Thu's gate (§12). Inside (§14, §15,
`sen_house_interior.jpg`): the doorframe without marks; one bowl and chopsticks at one place; the family altar table
with **the ancestors' photographs** and the letter folded small beneath them; the old radio hissing; the back room
with Thu's chair facing the door and the dusty teapot and cups; the half-finished paper fish on the shelf. Out the
back: the back step (§6), the yard and its fence, a gap in the rocks, the **seven stone steps** (M5 decision) down to
the cove.

**The cove and the bottom step** (`5_the_bottom_step.jpg`): mossy steps between rocks, the house's back wall above
you, the sandals lined up (§14), the carp coming up, and through the gap **the whole lake**: the lanterns gathered in
front of the stage, the village's lights, the far shore (§4). The game's most important view.

**The far shore** (`8_far_shore_festival.jpg`): the stage on its stilts, the pavilion, the banners, the flooded path,
the karst peaks. Seen, never reached.

**Behind the village:** the hedge, the wild rice fields with the graves (`6_village_edge_graves.jpg`), the banyan, the
roadside shrine, the forest (`FOREST_PROPOSAL.md`), the cliffs.

## 7. Decided from the references (2026-11-02; Catlyn: "make of it what you will")

- **Layout:** Catlyn's map (§2) replaces the ring: the village is a cluster on the east shore with two roads; the
  shrine alone on the west point; the bridge across the bay.
- **Sen's house is two storeys**, on the headland (v2 `4_the_last_lane.jpg`); the dock view (`7_…`) shows it smaller
  because it's far away and from the side.
- **Lotus lanterns for every soul; a paper carp only for Sen** (§6).
- **The carp at the steps glow faintly**, pale ghost carp (`5_the_bottom_step.jpg`).
- **Karst mountains** make the edge of the world (north-central Vietnam: Quảng Bình, Ninh Bình).
- **The altar keeps the story:** the ancestors' photographs (adults; never Sen: she had no photo, §14), the letter
  hidden under them, found with "Look under the altar". The images' bowl on the altar and envelope in plain sight
  follow the story instead (bowl at the table, letter folded small and hidden).
- **The sandals:** one pair per lost child (§14): Minh, Hạnh, Tí, Khoa, and **Bin's** (taken before the players reach
  the steps). The images show more; five pairs. DRAFT: Catlyn to confirm Bin's.
