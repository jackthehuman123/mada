# Release Council: ethics + market seats, Làng Vọng (Mada), 2026-11-04

## Ethics
**Content (read from Text.luau, Dialogue.luau, Clues.luau, Config.SenJumpscare).**
- Themes: children who drowned (four named boys/girls, "the lake took the children"), a neglected/isolated girl implied to be abused or shut away (teacher's report, "father keeps wife and daughter away", empty doorframe), a grieving mother in denial. Nothing graphic: no gore, no blood, no on-screen harm to a child, no weapons. Death is handled through objects and implication.
- Fear: a face-under-water jumpscare with a sting, a chase (Sen walks out of the water), "taken" screens, a bad ending (everyone hunted until morning, Bin never found).
- Likely Content Maturity label: **Moderate** (Roblox's "Moderate" covers moderate violence/fear, infrequent intense moments, with the jumpscare and drowned-children theme pushing it above Mild). Not Restricted: no gore, sexual content, or realistic violence. Answer the questionnaire honestly on "fear/horror" and "death"; do not try to land on Minimal/Mild. Moderate gates the game from under-9 accounts, which suits it.
- Vietnamese beliefs: treated respectfully. Incense, altar, Rằm tháng bảy lanterns, the name written for the dead are framed as acts of care, and the resolution is giving Sen rest and a name, not destroying her. Risks: (1) incense used as a gameplay "repel for a minute" tool and refilled like a consumable is mildly flippant; (2) the bad ending is the ghost punishing everyone, fine for horror. No mockery found. Catlyn, as the Vietnamese author, has approved most text; the lines marked DRAFT (Goals, Rules, Recap, Menu, takenSolo, wayBack) are still unreviewed.
- Pressure/deception: none found. No purchases, timers pushing spending, or fake urgency. The "Taken" mechanic is in-fiction. Dialogue never addresses the real player manipulatively.
- Data: Analytics.luau is an in-memory stub that only prints; nothing is sent, no DataStore use found for personal data. Roblox's own platform analytics apply. Fine.
- Chat/invites: default Roblox chat is untouched (no custom chat found). The menu has Open/Private teams with Invite friends. Open teams let strangers (including adults) join under-16 players; platform chat filtering and age-checked chat apply, but the menu does not warn. Group-only gate (5099606) makes this moot for the playtest.

**Before a group-only playtest:** nothing must change. Tell testers (and their guardians, if under 13) that it is a scary game with a jumpscare.
**Before public:** (1) complete the maturity questionnaire as Moderate and set the experience description to name the scares and the drowned-children theme; (2) a fear/jumpscare warning on the menu ("contains sudden scares and themes of drowned children") and a setting to turn off the face jumpscare (Config.SenJumpscare exists, expose it to the player; add a flash/loud-sound note); (3) Catlyn reviews all DRAFT strings; (4) default new teams to Private, or label Open teams as "may include players you don't know"; (5) if analytics are forwarded later, use AnalyticsService only (no personal data).

## Market
**Who it is for:** 13-17 year olds who watch horror YouTubers and play Roblox horror with friends (Vietnamese and SEA-leaning, folklore interest, headphone/camcorder-found-footage fans), in groups of 2-4. Story-first, slow-burn, not a chase-and-escape arcade. Comparable titles: Doors, The Mimic, Piggy, Dead Silence, Apeirophobia, Flee the Facility. They give a threat and a reason to move in the first 60-120 seconds (a monster seen or heard, a door to open, a first scare), shareable moments (a rare entity, a death message, a funny failure), visible progression (rooms, floors, a counter), cosmetics/badges to chase, and fast restarts with randomized layouts so a run differs. Làng Vọng's first two minutes are a walk down a lane with Bin, then a long dialogue with Bà Thu (nine lines, skippable), with the first real threat much later. It has one fixed story with two endings, no random element, no badges, and the replay reason is only the bad ending vs. the good one. Co-op hooks are good in theory (the taken player is rescued with incense), but the "alone by the water" rule only bites when players split up.

**Single change most likely to lift day-1 retention:** put a scare and a decision inside the first 90 seconds, before Bà Thu. For example, on the lane Sen's hand or a lantern-lit glimpse of her at the water's edge, plus Bin's tape showing the rule, so a new team sees what the threat is and feels it before any dialogue. Badges for each ending and "found all clues" (cheap, and they give the 13-17 audience a replay goal) are the runner-up.

## Verdict (group-only playtest): **SHIP**
Blockers for group-only: none.
Blockers before public: maturity label + description wording (Moderate), visible scare warning and a jumpscare off-switch, Catlyn's review of DRAFT text, Open-team default/label, first-90-seconds threat for retention.
