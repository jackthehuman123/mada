# Round 1 - Art Director

**Score: 4/10** against a popular Roblox horror story. The mood is right (moon, stars, fog, lit windows, lantern flotilla). The things the player looks at are not: the shrine, the figures and the interiors are graybox.

Scene scores: lane 5, village at dusk with lit windows (06_night1-lit-windows) 6, shrine (02_world-shrine, 04_camcorder-*) 3, carp steps / causeway 4, house interiors (07_mystery-*) 5, drowned festival (08_sen-drowned-festival) 6 for the lanterns / 2 for the figures, Bin dialogue (06_night1-dialogue) 2, endings 3, UI 6.

1. **Shrine is the most-seen hero object and reads as red slabs. DESIGN (art pass, owner approves the spend).** 02_world-shrine and 04_camcorder-viewfinder-live show flat saturated orange-red columns, a red box altar, a brown plank and a red cube as the offering. Neon-red untextured wood next to a muted world looks like a placeholder, not a shrine. Fix: model the shrine in Blender (roof with curved eaves, carved posts, an altar with a real incense bowl, a framed photo). If that is not possible this week, desaturate the red to a lacquer maroon (~120,28,24) and replace the box altar.
2. **Figures are boxes with ball heads. BUG-level art debt; "model in Blender" is the fix.** `Figures.luau` `piece()` builds everything from Block/Ball parts. `Figures.Child` (lines 75-86) is one 1.3-wide box plus a ball: in 08_sen-drowned-festival the "lost children" look like chess pawns or tombstones. `Figures.Sen` is a box body, two stick arms, a ball head and a hair block. `Figures.Hand` is four 0.07 bars. Fix: upload one rigged-or-static child mesh (silhouette is enough: head, shoulders, tapering body, a few arm variants) and a Sen mesh (áo bà ba, long wet hair strands, small hands); keep the same function signatures so tests do not change.
3. **The jumpscare face does not land. BUG/art.** `Figures.SenFace` is a ball with two 0.17-wide slits and slab hair curtains. At the 4-5 stud range it will read as a Roblox head with a black fringe. 09_endings-ending-silence and 08 show only grey scanline bars (test-artifact frames; cannot judge). Fix: a hair-curtain mesh with strands, an actual pale face texture (decal on a head mesh, large dark eyes, no mouth), and a slow drift plus a one-frame cut to full screen.
4. **Darkness is uneven: either pitch black or flat.** 02_world-top-down, 06_night1-flooded-causeway, 09_endings-ending-home and 09_endings-write-sen are near fully black; 02_world-lane and 02_world-sen-house are readable but dull olive green with no focal light. `Build.luau:644-662` sets Ambient (64,72,98) with fog 40-420, so exteriors are lit evenly while interiors and the underwater look jump to black. Fix: keep the exterior, add warm point lights in each house interior (the "lit windows" frame proves it works, 06_night1-lit-windows), and raise the camcorder's minimum exposure so tapes never go black (06_night1-tape2-under is a black frame with a HUD; may be a test artifact). BUG.
5. **Interior walls look wrong in 06_night1-dialogue.** The Bà Thu dialogue frame shows full-screen magenta walls with dark wedges: either the camera is clipped into a house, or a missing-material colour. It may be a mid-transition test frame, but check what Color3 the house walls get. BUG to verify.
6. **Flat grass terrain.** 02_world-lane and 02_world-sen-house show one tiling green plane, no paths worn into it, no fences, no rice/vegetable plots, a single palm. The village is five identical white-plaster houses with red roofs. Fix: Blender-modelled props (the owner already did trees, crops and furniture): fish racks, a boat, fences, a well, a bridge with railings, hanging lanterns in the houses. Break up the ground with dirt-path decals and mud around the water edge.
7. **Reeds and the causeway are placeholder bars.** 07_mystery-carp-steps / 02_world-carp-steps: the steps are a flat grey block, the causeway a black strip, the reeds (02_world-shrine, 06_night1-bin-camera) thin tilted sticks. Fix: reed-cluster mesh with alpha leaves and a stone causeway mesh with moss.
8. **Lantern glow is the right idea but too uniform.** The lanterns are identical yellow balls (04, 06, 08). Fix: use paper-lantern meshes (red/amber), flicker, a warm light cone on the water.
9. **UI.** Journal (J) button, red Close and body text are readable and match the world. Issues: the default Roblox chat hint ("Chat '/?'...") sits at top left on every frame, which breaks immersion; hide it. Subtitles at the bottom are small and thin on bright areas (06_night1-tape1). Add a dark backing strip.
10. **Camcorder overlay is good.** Keep it. Only the tape contents (see 2, 3) let it down.

**Summary of fixes by effort:** (a) colour/light changes in `Build.luau`, hide chat hint, interior lights: small. (b) Blender meshes for Child, Sen, Sen face and hand, shrine, reeds, causeway: the main work, and it decides whether the game looks published-quality.
