# Plumber, round 1 (27 frames; report.md all PASS)

Code pointers are by file, not verified line numbers.

1. **06_night1-dialogue: camera inside Bà Thu (BUG).** The frame is hot-magenta wall plus dark brown slabs, with the dialogue box over geometry. The camera sits inside her body or the altar. Cause: the dialogue camera is placed relative to the NPC with no occlusion check. Fix: raycast from her head to the camera and pull it in front, or use a fixed offset about 5 studs out.
2. **Shrine colours and scale (BUG).** Pillars, altar and banner are flat saturated red/orange plastic (02_world-shrine, 04_camcorder-*, 06_night1-tape1, 07_mystery-memento; the magenta in #1 is the same family). The altar is a giant red box next to a head-sized glow ball. Fix in Build.luau: desaturated lacquer (about 120,35,30), real altar proportions, remove the pink.
3. **Lantern glow balls (BUG).** The Neon PaperLantern spheres (Build.luau:81, Size 1.4) render as huge flat yellow discs covering a third of the frame (04_camcorder-viewfinder-live, 06_night1-tape1, 02_world-shrine). 09_endings-finale-home is 40+ identical blobs with no lantern shape and no light falloff. Fix: a lantern mesh/cylinder with a PointLight, dimmer Neon, and lower Bloom.
4. **Camcorder frames are an orange wash (BUG).** 04_camcorder-* and 06_night1-tape1 show no night-vision look; the sandy floor is unshaded orange. The chat hint and the "Journal (J)" button sit on the viewfinder corner brackets (top-left, bottom-left). Fix: hide the chat hint and the Journal button while the viewfinder is up (UIController.luau, viewfinder around line 285) and darken the tint.
5. **06_night1-tape2: the girl in the water is a black cube head on a white box (BUG).** It reads as a placeholder, not a ghost. Fix: a Sen model with hair and a dress, with a distinct silhouette per tape beat.
6. **Carp steps (02_world-carp-steps, 07_mystery-carp-steps) (BUG).** The steps are grey blocks in a straight-edged rectangular notch of water, the path to them is a pure black strip with a hard edge (it looks like a hole), and the "carp" are flat blue slabs. Fix: a stone texture, a curved shoreline, a path material, and orange/white fish.
7. **Water and shore edges (BUG).** There are straight polygon edges in the water (02_world-shrine right, 02_world-lane left, 02_world-carp-steps). Palm trees are thin sticks with a few green planes (02_world-lane). Grass is one flat plane with no variation. The houses are the best-looking element, but they are copies with flat roofs (06_night1-lit-windows).
8. **UI text contrast (BUG).** The bottom message "Find Bin." and "The causeway is under water." are thin, semi-transparent and nearly invisible on dark frames (06_night1-flooded-causeway, 09_endings-write-sen, 06_night1-bin-camera). On 09_endings-ending-home the ending text is dark grey on black and the Journal button is faded, so it is unreadable at the very end of the game. Fix: text colour at least 220 with a dark backing bar, and finish the fade before the text appears.
9. **Journal panel (03_clues-journal).** A large empty beige rectangle (about 420x460) covers a third of the view with small text. Fix: size it to its content. The clue panels (03_clues-clue-panel, 07_mystery-*) are readable and centred, but sit over the key prop in 07_mystery-sen-house. Confirm the panel closes when walking away or when Sen warns.
10. **Interior scale (07_mystery-sen-house, 07_mystery-teacher-house).** Rooms are small and plain, and furniture is thin slabs. The wardrobe and bench look undersized next to a character, but no frame shows the character for scale. Add a scale shot.
11. **Unclear whether stylised or a fault:** 06_night1-tape2-hand and 09_endings-ending-silence are full-screen horizontal grey bars with no readable content. If that is the intended static, keep a faint image under it (06_night1-tape2-hand does show the steps through the bars; ending-silence shows nothing).

**Probably test artifacts:** 02_world-top-down (almost black, teleported camera; the lack of ambient light is still real), 06_night1-flooded-causeway (mid fade, though the straight-edged flood polygon is real), 06_night1-tape2-under (black with HUD only), 08_sen-drowned-festival (black frame with the lantern button), 03_clues-journal (black scene behind the panel), 07_* letter panel left open on three different scenes (the test reuses the panel).

Plumber's verdict: the exterior houses at night are acceptable, and the shrine, Bà Thu, Sen, water edges and camcorder look are not release quality. Art 3/10, UI legibility 5/10.
