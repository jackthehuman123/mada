# Timeline

To discuss. What's fixed so far:

1. **Intro:** the rumor video the group watched.
2. **Dusk on the 14th of ghost month:** the group arrives.
3. **Night 1 (about 20 minutes, the same every time):** Bà Thu, the lanterns drifting to the houses, Bin vanishes, his camera.
4. **Open:** everything between Night 1 and the finale.
5. **Midnight on the 15th (Rằm tháng bảy):** the finale.
6. **Dawn:** the ending.

## Three possible shapes

| Option | How it works | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One continuous night | Night 1 runs straight into the finale; a clock counts toward midnight | Tense and simple, but only one night of lanterns to watch |
| Night, day, night | Night 1, then the day of the 15th (calmer, for exploring houses and reading clues), then the final night | Natural rhythm, and players see the lanterns drift twice, but the day needs its own reason to be scary |
| Rest skips time | Time passes only when players rest at the shrine | Players control the pace, but there's less pressure |

## Questions to discuss

- Should players be able to watch the lanterns drift more than once (they're key to the puzzle)?
- When does the Silence spread: over time, or as players learn more?
- What happens if players reach midnight with only half the story?
