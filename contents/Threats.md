# Threats

To discuss. The guiding idea: every threat should be a piece of the village's silence, not a random monster. These are the current ideas.

## Sen, the ma da

She takes whoever is alone near the water, the way she was. She's usually seen as a reflection, a shape among the carp, or a lantern moving against the wind.

| Warning sign | What it means | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A child crying near the water | She's luring someone | Never go toward it alone |
| A lit lantern drifting toward you | Bait | Never reach into the water for it |
| Carp gathering at the edge near you | She's right below | Step away from the water |
| Small wet footprints following you (visible only on camera) | She's chosen you because you're alone | Get back to your group fast |
| A children's rhyme hummed with your name in it | She's about to grab | Light incense, or run to the shrine |

## The Silence and Sen's father

After he went into the lake, Sen wouldn't let him near her. He's stuck in the houses, inside the silence he chose that night. Silence spreads out from Sen's house; inside it, all ambient sound dies, and he hunts by sound.

| Warning sign | What it means | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A radio crackles on and gets louder | He's coming | Hide the way Sen used to: under a bed, in a cupboard, behind the water jar |
| A floorboard creaks nearby | He's in the room | Don't move; walking makes noise |
| A door slams behind you | He's blocking the way out | Wait until the radio fades |

## Smaller dangers

- **Hungry ghosts (cô hồn):** wander the paths during ghost month and snatch what you carry. Rice and salt keep them off.
- **Protective items:** incense sticks, rice and salt.
